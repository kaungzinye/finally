# Board

The Tasks tab's column view, grouping tasks by status: Not started, In progress, Complete.

The Board shows each [[Task]] as a card with its priority ring.

## Rules

- Cards in the window between scheduled day and deadline carry the orange deadline tint.
- Columns grow only as tall as their cards.
- A status bar across the top keeps all three columns reachable without scrolling sideways.
- Cards drag between columns, and their status chip cycles on tap.
