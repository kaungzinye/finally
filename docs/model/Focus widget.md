# Focus widget

Medium home screen widget. The Daily Focus list, headed "3 things to do".

The focus widget lists the open picks of the [[Daily Focus]]. Finished picks drop off.

## Rules

- Tapping a ring finishes the task from the home screen.
- While the [[Personal agent]]'s proposed Daily Focus waits for confirmation, the focus widget lists the proposed titles under "3 things to confirm" without rings, and a tap opens [[Today tab]].
